Look closely. Rank freely.

  1. Compare the same photograph. Each set contains three encoder versions at the same distance setting. The study has 31 photographs at three settings. It is a comparison at matched distance, not matched file size.
  2. Arrange best to worst. Drag the sample headers, use the arrow buttons, or focus a header and press the arrow keys. On a small screen, best is at the top. Mark ties if the difference is invisible.
  3. Inspect the details. Zoom and pan stay synchronized. At 100%, one image pixel occupies one CSS pixel. Inspect gives a larger view: 1 2 3 switch samples; R shows the source reference when enabled in Settings. Esc closes it.
  4. Submit deliberately. Save ranking & next confirms the current order, including an unchanged order. Drafts autosave but are excluded from CSV. Revisiting a ranked image keeps the previous submission until you save again.
  5. Keep your results. Export CSV contains submitted rankings and the real encoder identities. Avoid reading the CSV until you finish. Backups in Settings also preserve unfinished work.

Images are full-resolution, 16-bit sRGB PNG decodes of the saved JPEG XL results. Reference images are the original PNGs. No uploads, analytics, external fonts, or network services.
